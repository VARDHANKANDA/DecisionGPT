### 1.5 Digital Twin price response: root-cause evidence (reproduced 2026-10-15, read-only probes)

The earlier statement "exactly flat" is **not reproduced in general**. What the code and probes show:

1. **Price does reach the model.** `digital_twin_service._scenario_inputs` computes `scenario_price = baseline_price * (1 + pct/100)`;
   `forecast_service.build_feature_row` places it unchanged in the `price` column; nothing clips or overwrites it. It is the only
   price pathway.
2. **The response is piecewise-constant and unsigned.** On 300 random training rows a +25% price move changed the prediction in 76%
   of rows with mean change **+0.49 units** (up, not down); on the demo business a 30-day total moved +71 units for +25% price, −5 for
   +10%, +0 for ±5%. Tree splits on `price` are few (271 splits, gain 8,021 against 1,004,583 for `lag_7`).
3. **Training data cannot identify price sensitivity.** Median **2 distinct prices per series** (within-series CV 3.3%); the pooled
   correlation of price with units is **+0.10**, while the within-series log-log slope is **−1.34**. Across series, price is a proxy for
   series identity (expensive series sell more), so the model learns "price = which series", not elasticity.
4. **Inference input differs in kind and scale.** Training: `units_sold` per series-day (mean 128). Production: business-wide *sum* of
   units (mean ~11) and `price = revenue / units`, a realised mix price rather than a product price. Lag features are far outside the
   training range, so leaf values near the low end are hit.
5. `promotion_flag` is hard-coded 0 at inference (`build_feature_row`); the scenario cannot express promotions.

**Consequence for phase 6:** unit tests will pin price propagation (which already holds) and report the response curves honestly. The
repair that is defensible is an *identification-aware* model path: (a) train with within-item price variation (M5 `sell_prices` has
real weekly price changes per item-store), (b) add item-relative price features (`price / item mean price`) so price cannot act as a
series ID, (c) report a within-item fixed-effects log-log estimate next to the model response and show "price sensitivity not
identified" where variation is insufficient. No elasticity will be imposed or forced monotone.

## 3. Evaluation protocol (applies to every task)

* **Chronological only.** No shuffling. A final untouched test window per dataset; touched once after selection is frozen.
* **Rolling origin** for selection: >= 3 forecast origins before the test window; scores aggregated across origins.
* **Information set** at origin `T`: target history up to `T`; calendar and (for M5) selling prices, which are known in advance; no
  future target-derived feature. Direct strategy: every feature for day `t` is built from days `<= t - H` (H = horizon).
* **Preprocessing fitted on training rows of each fold only.** (Tree models need none; the linear models use a `StandardScaler`
  inside the pipeline.)
* **Metrics:** MAE, RMSE, **WAPE** = sum|e| / sum y (undefined when sum y = 0: reported as `null`, never 0), bias; R² only for the
  aggregate daily series where variance is non-zero. Per-series and aggregate results. Intermittent demand: share of zero days and
  per-series WAPE reported; scale-free per-series metric is RMSSE against a seasonal-naive in-sample scale (undefined for constant
  history: excluded and counted).
* **Uncertainty:** paired differences on identical (series, day) pairs; **moving-block bootstrap by origin/week** to respect temporal
  dependence; effect sizes reported, overlapping CIs are not used as a test.
* **Selection:** hyper-parameters and ensembles chosen on validation origins only; a fixed small documented grid (no test-set tuning).
* **Run metadata:** dataset id and checksum, split boundaries, seeds, parameters, feature list, package versions, git revision, runtime.

## 5. Task definitions

### F1 — M5 daily unit sales, H = 28 days
* **Subset (RAM-bound, seeded):** store `CA_1` (all 7 departments), stratified random sample of 350 item-store series by department
  with seed 20261015, requiring >= 365 days of history after the first non-zero sale. Selection rule and series list are stored.
* **Windows:** test = last 28 days (d_1886–d_1913); validation origins = the three preceding 28-day windows; train = everything
  before each origin. Final models are refit on data up to the test origin using the hyper-parameters chosen on validation.
* **Features (all <= t − 28):** lag_28/35/42/56, rolling mean/std of the 28-lagged series (7/28/84 windows), same-weekday mean of the
  last 4 weeks; known-in-advance: weekday, month, day-of-month, event flags, SNAP, `sell_price`, price relative to the item's
  trailing median, weeks since price change; categorical item/dept as integer codes (CatBoost native).
* **Baselines:** seasonal naive (value at t − 28, same weekday), same-weekday 4-week mean.

### F2 — Online Retail II, single real business
* **Targets:** (a) total daily revenue, (b) units for the 20 highest-revenue SKUs (cancellations and non-product codes removed by a
  documented rule). H = 7 and 28 days; same rolling-origin scheme; test = last 28 days.
* Real but small (~600 trading days, strong December seasonality, a single December in test: reported as a limitation).

### C1 — customer inactivity on Online Retail II (**proxy label, not observed churn**)
Online Retail II has no churn field (no contracts). A customer-level label "no purchase in the 90 days after the cutoff" is built
with a **temporal** design: features from transactions strictly before cutoff `T`, label from `(T, T + 90d]`, three cutoffs
(train / validation / test, non-overlapping label windows). It is reported as *purchase-inactivity prediction on a real retailer*, never as churn. The
synthetic `platform-churn-v1` results stay separate and are described as synthetic.

### P1 — price-response identification (Digital Twin)
On M5 CA_1: within-item fixed-effects log-log regression (item and week-of-year effects, event/SNAP controls, item-clustered SEs) to
estimate association between relative price and units; compare with the response of each candidate model to a ±10% price
counterfactual on the same rows. Reported as predictive association; promotions and availability are not randomised, so no causal
claim.

## 7. Risks and stopping rules

* **Compute/RAM:** run LightGBM/CatBoost with `n_jobs=2`/`thread_count=2`; if a task exceeds ~45 min or memory, reduce the series
  sample (documented) instead of changing the protocol.
* **Negative results are reported** (a model not beating seasonal-naive, ensembles not helping, price not identified).
* **Licence/provenance:** the M5 copy's licence label is the uploader's; the report says so. If it is contested, the M5 results are
  to be removed from any publication.
* **No live provider traffic:** all experiments are local; offline isolation is kept (tests block outbound HTTP).
* Historical runs (`experiments/model_comparison_v1`, `experiments/r1`, `upgraded_controlled_v*`, `models/registry_index.jsonl`
  entries, `research/llm_evaluation/runs`) are not modified; new work writes only to `experiments/ml_benchmark_v1/` and
  `data/external/*`.

## 9. Status and deviations from this plan (added 2026-10-15, after the runs)

Executed as planned: F1 (M5), F2 (Online Retail II), C1 (inactivity proxy), C2 (synthetic churn), P1 (price identification), the
reproduce task, validation-based selection, WAPE, run metadata and the Digital Twin limitation notes. Results: `docs/ML_BENCHMARK_REPORT.md`.

Deviations (all recorded in the report):
* **Forecast stacking meta-model not run** (planned "only if sufficient independent validation data"): three overlapping-series validation
  origins are not enough; NNLS weighting was used instead.
* **C2 first attempt superseded**: it used columns the production churn model excludes; re-run with the production feature set. The first run is
  kept with a `SUPERSEDED.md` and is not used in the report.
* **Compute reductions**: random forest 100 trees with 30% sub-sampling; CatBoost 500 iterations; 350 of 3,049 M5 series of one store.
* **Single horizon for F2** (28 days): a direct H=7 model cannot be scored over a 28-day window without using information that is not
  available at the origin.
* **Phase 7 partial by design**: selection logic, metrics and metadata are shared; production feature sets and artifact versions were not
  rewritten, and no production model was retrained (the trainers would overwrite version `v1`).
* **Plan section 1.5 correction**: the earlier "exactly flat" description of the Digital Twin price response was not reproduced in general;
  the measured behaviour is piecewise-constant, small and unsigned (report section 6).
