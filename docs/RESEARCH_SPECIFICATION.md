# DecisionGPT — Research Specification

## 1. Research Title

**DecisionGPT: A Goal-Driven Multi-Agent AI Framework with Business Digital Twin and Causal Reasoning for Explainable Strategic Decision Support in Indian SMEs**

## 2. Research Problem

Indian SMEs often have fragmented business data but limited access to advanced strategic decision support.

Existing tools commonly provide descriptive and predictive analytics but do not fully connect:
- goals;
- predictions;
- interventions;
- simulation;
- multi-perspective evaluation;
- explanation.

## 3. Research Gap

DecisionGPT investigates the integration of:
- goal-driven planning;
- predictive analytics;
- Digital Twin simulation;
- causal reasoning;
- multi-agent evaluation;
- explainability.

The contribution is the integrated decision pipeline, not any single model.

## 4. Main Research Question

Can a goal-driven multi-agent AI framework integrating predictive analytics, Business Digital Twin simulation and causal reasoning improve strategic decision support for Indian SMEs compared with conventional predictive analytics?

## 5. Secondary Questions

RQ1. Does Digital Twin simulation improve strategy selection over prediction-only approaches?

RQ2. Does multi-agent evaluation improve strategy evaluation over a single-agent approach?

RQ3. Does causal reasoning improve intervention explanations and strategy quality?

RQ4. Does explainability improve user understanding?

## 6. Contributions

1. Goal-to-decision planning.
2. Business Digital Twin for strategy simulation.
3. Evidence-labelled causal reasoning.
4. Multi-agent strategy evaluation.
5. Explainable, risk-aware recommendations.
6. Decision memory and outcome tracking.

## 7. Hypotheses

H1: Digital Twin-enhanced decision support improves strategy selection over prediction-only support.

H2: Multi-agent evaluation improves strategy evaluation over a single-agent baseline.

H3: The full system improves goal-aligned strategy performance over baselines.

H4: Explainable recommendations improve user understanding.

## 8. Baselines

A. Prediction + rule-based recommendation.

B. Prediction + Digital Twin.

C. Prediction + Digital Twin + single agent.

D. Full DecisionGPT.

## 9. Evaluation

Forecasting:
- MAE
- RMSE
- MAPE

Classification:
- Precision
- Recall
- F1
- ROC-AUC

Decision:
- goal achievement;
- expected benefit;
- risk-adjusted score;
- strategy selection quality.

Explainability:
- explanation consistency;
- user comprehension;
- perceived usefulness.

## 10. Ablation

Remove one major component at a time:
- Digital Twin
- Causal Graph
- Multi-Agent Engine
- Explainability
- Memory

## 11. Research Integrity

No result is entered into the paper until produced by a recorded experiment.

## 12. Limitations

- Public datasets may not represent every Indian SME.
- Counterfactual estimates depend on model assumptions.
- Causal inference may be limited by observational data.
- LLM outputs may vary.
- Human evaluation may be small.
