# Multi-Scenario Experiment Protocol

Strengthens **Table 4 (Decision Architecture Comparison)** and **Table 5
(Ablation Study)** from `1 scenario × 1 seed` to
`12 scenarios × 5 seeds = 60 paired evaluations` per architecture / config.

**No architecture change.** `multi_scenario_service` only adds *scenario
generation* + *statistical aggregation*; it calls the existing
`decision_architecture_service` / `ablation_service` code unchanged (same
architectures A–D, same ablation meanings A–F, same production
`decision_service.analyze_goal`). The original single-scenario runs are
preserved untouched as `legacy_single_scenario_result` (experiment types
`decision_architecture` / `ablation`).

## Scenarios (deterministic, stable IDs S01–S12)

Generated by `multi_scenario_service.SCENARIOS`, threaded into the existing
`decision_architecture_service._seed_synthetic_business(db, seed, scenario)`.
Each scenario is an *ephemeral synthetic research business* — created fresh,
deleted after the run, never a real SME.

| ID | Business type | Goal | Primary KPI | Target % | Lever | Notable economics |
|---|---|---|---|---:|---|---|
| S01 | Apparel Retail | increase_revenue | revenue | 12 | pricing | high margin (cost 200 / price 600), low elasticity |
| S02 | Apparel Retail | increase_profit | profit | 12 | pricing | same economics as S01, profit goal |
| S03 | Electronics Retail | increase_sales | orders | 15 | marketing | high ticket, low demand, strong marketing effect |
| S04 | Grocery Retail | reduce_inventory_risk | inventory_risk* | 10 | inventory | thin margin, high demand, **+ inventory records** |
| S05 | Furniture Retail | increase_profit | profit | 15 | pricing | very high ticket (6k/12k), tiny demand |
| S06 | E-commerce | increase_revenue | revenue | 20 | marketing | mid margin, elastic demand, aggressive target |
| S07 | Apparel E-commerce | improve_marketing_roi | marketing_roi* | 15 | marketing | strong marketing effectiveness |
| S08 | Consumer Goods | increase_profit | profit | 10 | inventory | very thin margin, high volume, **+ inventory** |
| S09 | Regional Retail | increase_sales | orders | 12 | pricing | **very price-sensitive** (promo uplift 12) |
| S10 | Small Manufacturing | increase_profit | profit | 8 | inventory/cost | **no marketing at all**, **+ inventory** |
| S11 | Mixed Retail | increase_revenue | revenue | 15 | marketing | mid economics, moderate elasticity |
| S12 | Small E-commerce | increase_revenue | revenue | 8 | conservative | small scale, modest target |

`*` `marketing_roi` and `inventory_risk` have no directly-simulated pair in
the Digital Twin output, so `goal_achievement` for those scenarios uses
**revenue attainment as a documented proxy** (see `STATISTICAL_ANALYSIS.md`).

**`reduce_churn` deliberately excluded** — the candidate-strategy grid has no
retention lever and the synthetic generator has no churn-shaped customer
data. Per the task's "replace an unsupported scenario" rule it is substituted
by additional supported scenarios and recorded here.

### Diversity achieved

- business type (12 distinct), baseline scale (₹99–₹36k line value),
  gross margin (≈0.10 grocery → ≈0.67 apparel), daily demand (1–3 furniture
  → 40–70 grocery), price sensitivity (promo uplift 1 → 12), marketing
  effectiveness (attributed revenue 0 → 16k), inventory position
  (3 scenarios carry stock records), goal (4 distinct objectives),
  target (8 %–20 %), horizon (2–3 months).

## Seeds

`[42, 43, 44, 45, 46]`. The per-(scenario, seed) synthetic business is seeded
with `random.Random(f"{scenario_id}:{seed}")`, so:
- the **same** `(scenario_id, seed)` always produces the **same** business
  (tested), and
- different seeds vary only the daily quantity / marketing noise, not the
  scenario's structure.

## Fairness

For one `(scenario, seed)` the business + goal are generated **once**; all four
architectures (and all six ablation configs) then run against that identical
state, the same `CANDIDATE_GRID`, the same seed, and the same 2–3 month
horizon. No architecture receives information another does not. Evaluation uses
only decision-time / simulated data — there are no "actual outcomes" in this
experiment, so future-outcome leakage is structurally impossible (tested).

## Metrics recorded per observation

`scenario_id, scenario_label, primary_lever, goal_objective, goal_primary_kpi,
kpi_measured, goal_target_percent, seed, architecture (A–D) / config (A–F),
selected_strategy, goal_achievement, risk_adjusted_score, confidence
(architecture D / ablation configs only), latency_seconds`,
plus `delta_*_vs_full` for the ablation.

`goal_achievement` = attainment of the scenario's **own** primary KPI
(revenue / profit / orders), computed identically for every architecture from
its selected strategy's simulated output. This is `decision_architecture_service._kpi_goal_achievement`
— a superset of the legacy revenue-only `_goal_achievement`, which the legacy
experiment still uses unchanged.

## Reproduce

```bash
export DATABASE_URL=sqlite:///./backend/dev.db
python backend/scripts/dev_bootstrap_sqlite.py
python -c "import sys;sys.path.insert(0,'backend');from app.db.session import SessionLocal;\
from app.services import model_registry_service as m;d=SessionLocal();m.sync_from_file_registry(d);d.close()"

curl -X POST localhost:8000/api/v1/research/experiments/run -H "X-Research-Token: $RT" \
  -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  -d '{"experiment_type":"multi_scenario_architecture","configuration":{"seeds":[42,43,44,45,46]}}'
curl -X POST localhost:8000/api/v1/research/experiments/run -H "X-Research-Token: $RT" \
  -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  -d '{"experiment_type":"multi_scenario_ablation","configuration":{"seeds":[42,43,44,45,46]}}'

curl -s localhost:8000/api/v1/research/experiments/manifest -H "X-Research-Token: $RT" \
  > experiments/experiment_manifest.json
```

Exports:
`decision_architecture` / `ablation` (aggregate Tables 4 / 5),
`decision_architecture_detail` / `ablation_detail` (every observation),
via `POST /api/v1/research/export {"table": "...", "format": "csv|json|markdown|latex"}`.
