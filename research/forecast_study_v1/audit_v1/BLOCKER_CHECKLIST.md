## B. Checks that passed (no action)

* Evaluation targets equal the raw data in all six cells; row sets identical across methods; no missing/negative targets.
* All 16 baselines re-implemented independently from raw data match on every row; matched/fresh cutoffs precede the target (gaps 7/28 and 1..H).
* 356 + 104 numeric comparisons against the manuscript (Tables 1-2 and Sections 11-15): all pass except two interval endpoints within bootstrap noise.
* All six H1 and H2 verdicts reproduce under an independently implemented bootstrap and under a different (delta-method) variance.
* Selection-before-test order, hash records, historical artifacts, manifest hash, regeneration of tables and figures.
* The L1 experiment and the customer-split replacement are labelled post hoc/exploratory and kept out of the confirmatory families.
* The manuscript states the negative primary result honestly, reports all six cells, does not claim superiority, and describes the classification target as inactivity.
