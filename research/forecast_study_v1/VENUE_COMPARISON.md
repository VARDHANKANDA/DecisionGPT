# Venue comparison and submission advice (forecast_study_v1)

**Status:** advice only. Nothing has been submitted, no account was created, no editor was contacted and no fee was paid. Facts below were read from the venue's own page on **2026-10-22** where stated; anything not verified is marked.

## Recommendation

| Role | Venue | Why it fits | Main risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Primary** | *International Journal of Forecasting* (Elsevier; official publication of the International Institute of Forecasters) | Verified on the journal's Aims & Scope page: "strong emphasis on empirical studies, evaluation activities, implementation research and ways of improving the practice of forecasting"; lists time-series, machine-learning and big-data forecasting among its topics; "encourages replication studies"; double-blind review; data and code published as online supplements; submission via Editorial Manager; "Supports open access"; the guide for authors requires a declaration of generative-AI use and CRediT roles. | High novelty bar: a reviewer may judge a protocol-focused empirical study as incremental. Mitigate by foregrounding the metric-dependence finding and the pre-registered negative result. |
| Alternative 1 | *Transactions on Machine Learning Research* (TMLR) | Verified on its page: evaluation emphasises "technical correctness over subjective significance"; double-blind on OpenReview; rolling submissions; length unrestricted but should be justified; arXiv posting allowed (must not link a named version in the submission); LaTeX template required; supplementary material optional (≤ 100 MB). The page mentions no fees. | Audience is ML rather than forecasting practitioners; reviewers may want deep/foundation-model baselines. |
| Alternative 2 | *PLOS ONE* | Verified on its criteria page: accepts negative and null results; replication-type work needs a clear rationale and citation of prior literature; data must be deposited in public repositories following community norms. | **Publication fee not verified** (the official fee page was blocked by a bot check and was not bypassed; third-party sources gave conflicting amounts). Sound-science review does not assess impact, so visibility to forecasters is lower. |

## Predatory-venue and name-alike cautions

* None of the three recommended venues was flagged by this check. Predatory status was judged only from the publishers' own pages and well-known standing, not from a blacklist lookup; the authors should still confirm indexing and publisher membership before submitting anywhere.
* **Name-alike warning:** a web search for "International Journal of Forecasting" also returned an unrelated journal titled "Forecasting" hosted at ejournal.radenintan.ac.id. It is not the Elsevier/IIF journal. Submit only through the venue URL verified above (sciencedirect.com → Submit your article → Editorial Manager).
* Journals that approach authors by unsolicited e-mail, promise rapid acceptance, or hide fees should be avoided.

4. For all: the authors must complete Section 20 (authors, funding, competing interests, licence confirmation for the two datasets).
