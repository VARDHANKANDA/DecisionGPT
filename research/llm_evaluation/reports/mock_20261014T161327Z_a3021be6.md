## 1. Research question
How well does the LLM interpretation layer of DecisionGPT's analytics path turn natural-language business questions into valid structured analytics requests that the deterministic engine executes to results matching an independently computed ground truth?

**Metric boundary.** The deterministic query engine is the numerical authority. The evaluated AI component is the typed-decision classification of the natural-language question (OpenAI: schema-constrained labels only); periods, products and limits are extracted by deterministic code, and every answer sentence is a deterministic template (no model writes text).

**Not evaluated.** This experiment evaluates the ability of the model's classification, together with deterministic extraction, to interpret natural-language business questions into valid structured analytics requests that lead to deterministic, correct calculations. It does NOT evaluate LLM arithmetic ability, causal reasoning, profit prediction, churn prediction, pricing optimisation, marketing optimisation, business outcomes, human decision quality, or SME representativeness.

## 2-3. Dataset and provenance limitation
Indian POS retail register, 2025-01-01 to 2025-03-31. Evaluation-compatible view derived from the approved dataset: 30,293 rows from 94,290 processed lines (view sha256 `65e06599613ab6b0...`). View rules: 1) keep lines whose quantity is a whole number > 0 (sales.quantity is an integer column); 2) keep lines whose recomputed amount (qty*rate) agrees with a printed amount column; 3) merge lines that share (date, product, unit price) by summing quantity (the production upload validator rejects duplicate rows; merging preserves total sales and total units exactly; when a merged group mixed GST slabs, the slab with the largest revenue in the group is kept). Consequence: 'one row' no longer means 'one bill line', so no task asks about individual sale entries.

- Provenance: the POS data appears to be real Indian POS data from its structure and content, but the uploader/store provenance is NOT independently verified. It is not an official or government dataset and no such claim is made.
- Licence: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 as recorded in the repository (non-commercial, share-alike, attribution).
- Representativeness: one store, 89 days (1 Jan-31 Mar 2025), one season. NOT representative of Indian SMEs or Indian retail generally.
- Data view: 36% of lines (weighed/fractional quantities) cannot be stored by the production schema and are excluded; identical repeat lines are merged because the production upload rejects duplicates. Totals and units of the retained lines are exact; row grain is not a bill line. The view retains 78.22% of the source revenue.

## 4-5. Task design and ground truth
27 pre-registered tasks (spec sha256 `98f370377280c730...`, frozen hash verified before and after the run), 5 independent repeats each. Ground truth is computed by code from the view (gt-1.0) and is never shown to any LLM; tolerances were fixed before execution. The 4 GST tasks are a separate supplementary track: the production data has no GST field, so a refusal is the correct behaviour and is not scored as a failure.

## 13. Limitations
- Provenance: the POS data appears to be real Indian POS data from its structure and content, but the uploader/store provenance is NOT independently verified. It is not an official or government dataset and no such claim is made.
- Licence: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 as recorded in the repository (non-commercial, share-alike, attribution).
- Representativeness: one store, 89 days (1 Jan-31 Mar 2025), one season. NOT representative of Indian SMEs or Indian retail generally.
- Data view: 36% of lines (weighed/fractional quantities) cannot be stored by the production schema and are excluded; identical repeat lines are merged because the production upload rejects duplicates. Totals and units of the retained lines are exact; row grain is not a bill line. The view retains 78.22% of the source revenue.
- Scope: objective descriptive analytics / question answering only. Nothing here evaluates churn, causal effects, pricing or marketing optimisation, forecasting, business outcomes, or human decision quality.
- Scoring is a deterministic text parser. Verbose answers containing several numbers can be over-credited by the 'contains the truth' rule; the stricter first-number metric is reported beside it. 'Unsupported claim' is not assessed automatically; raw outputs are preserved for audit.
- No sampling parameter or seed is set; repeated requests are independent samples, not seeded replays. The client retries only rate-limit / connection errors, at most once; a timeout is never retried.
- One user question needs one classification request and, if the validated explanation is enabled, one explanation request. Tokens, latency and cost are reported only when the provider returned them (cost is an estimate from published prices, not a billing record).
- The reference tool-use system (a model that writes pandas code) needs text generation and was not run; no comparison with it exists in this run.
- A structured-request EXACT-MATCH accuracy is not available: the frozen task spec contains no gold structured requests. The recorded proxies are request validity, request execution, and agreement of the executed result with the independently computed ground truth.

## 14-15. What the results do / do not support
They describe how this LLM interpretation layer, with the deterministic engine behind it, handled these pre-registered objective questions over this one store's 89 days. They do not show agreement on other businesses, other question types, forecasting, churn, causal or pricing decisions, business outcomes, or human decision quality.
